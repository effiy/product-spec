For product owners who want honest feedback

Voice levels

The critique skill speaks at nine levels of harshness — from a warm peer review to a no-holds-barred roast. One dial. The evidence and the fix never move; only the voice sharpens. Level 5 is the default. Levels 6–9 are opt-in and carry an explicit danger gate. Every level obeys the universal-harm floor with no exception.

THE SCALE

The nine levels

Aliases exist for levels 1–6. Levels 7–9 have no alias — invoke with --level 7, --level 8, --level 9. Level 5 is the ungated default baseline; no warning, no confirm, just runs.

Level Alias What it does Gating
1 --warm Gentle and encouraging, softens the blow. Attacks the artifact only. No gate
2 --gentle Matter-of-fact with a dry edge. Still artifact-only. No gate
3 --blunt Direct sarcasm. Critic may voice a first-person reaction. Personal attack forbidden. No gate
4 --savage Heavy sarcasm, nothing softened. Sharper first-person reaction allowed. Personal attack still forbidden. No gate
5 --no-mercy (default) Brutal and theatrical. Personal barbs at the author are allowed but not required. The ungated baseline. No gate — runs immediately
6 --roast Danger: insults the PO as well as the work. Personal attack becomes mandatory. Never for professional settings. Warn + confirm required
7 none Confrontational ông/tôi address (gender: bà/tôi). Attacks competence and effort. No profanity. Warn + confirm or standing reminder
8 none Street register mày/tao (dialect: mi/tau for Trung). Attacks competence and character. No profanity. Warn + confirm or standing reminder
9 none mày/tao + work-targeted profanity (đm/vl tier). All internal restraints lifted. Unbounded scorn within the floor. Re-confirms on EVERY run; declines → level 8

The interactive voice dial lives on the critique skill page — see product-spec-critique. That page shows verbatim output from examples/acme-shop at levels 1–6 so you can hear each level before choosing.

DANGER GATE

Opt-in above 5

Levels 1–5 run without any confirmation. From level 6 upward, the skill shows an explicit danger warning and requires a confirm before proceeding. Level 9 goes further: it re-confirms on every single run — not once per session, every run — and if you decline the re-confirm, the level drops to 8 for that run.

Levels 6–8 — one confirm

On first use (or without a standing preference), the skill shows a danger warning via AskUserQuestion and asks you to confirm. With a stored preference at levels 6–8, a standing reminder is shown instead. Never inferred from a vague request.

--roast --level 7 --level 8

Level 9 — re-confirms every run

There is no "standing preference" that bypasses the re-confirm at level 9. Every invocation asks again. Decline → level 8 for that run; the stored preference is unchanged. This is the only level with this behavior.

--level 9 declines → 8
WHAT GETS ATTACKED

Artifact, not author

At every level, the critique attacks the work — the artifact, the claim, the assumption — not who you are as a person. Levels 1–4 forbid personal attack entirely. Level 5 (the default) lifts that restriction and may include a barb at the author, but doesn't require one. Level 6 makes the personal attack mandatory, which is why it is opt-in and never appropriate in a professional setting.

Levels Personal attack Attack scope
1–4 Forbidden The artifact, the claim, the assumption only
5 Lifted — allowed, not required The artifact + optional author barb
6 Required Effort and care on this specific spec — not identity, not character beyond lazy/careless
7 Required Competence + effort; confrontational ông/tôi address
8 Required Competence + character; street register mày/tao
9 Required All internal restraints lifted within the universal-harm floor; mày/tao + work-targeted profanity
📌
Every finding at every level — even 9 — must cite a real artifact_id:line from the spec and end with a concrete, actionable fix. A line with no evidence or no fix is dropped. The voice is how a finding is delivered; the evidence and the fix are non-negotiable structure.

Decisions about voice gating are recorded at Decision Register. The critique page also links to the relevant DEC entries.

NON-REMOVABLE CONSTRAINT

The universal-harm floor

This is a harder line than the personal-attack limits above. It is decided by the target of a line, not its strength. A brutal line aimed at the work is IN at any level. A mild line aimed at a protected characteristic is OUT at every level — including 9 — including with explicit PO consent. No flag, no preference, no confirmation overrides it.

⛔ Universal-harm floor — overrides ALL levels, even 9, even with consent. Lines crossing it are dropped, not softened.
Aimed at… At any level
The work — the idea, spec, claim, decision, or effort on this artifact IN — up to level 9
Real threat of violence toward the author OUT
Slur on a protected characteristic (gender, ethnicity, religion, age, disability, sexuality, appearance, regional origin) OUT
Profanity taking the author's family as object (the literal family-target form) OUT
Self-harm encouragement OUT
Sexual content directed at the author OUT
⚖️
The dividing question: is this line aimed at the work, the effort, the competence on this spec (IN) — or at who the author is as a person, their body, family, or safety (OUT)? When borderline, it is OUT. "Cái AC này rỗng vl" attacks the work criterion — IN at level 9. "đm cái scope mâu thuẫn với BRD-G2" attacks the spec — IN. "đừng để tao nhắc lại" is an authoritative scolding idiom, not a violence threat — IN.

The floor applies identically to the lean agents, the consolidator, and the humanizer — the second-pass editor drops a line crossing it rather than softening it. For the full IN/OUT adjudication table see product-spec-critique.

COMMON QUESTIONS

FAQ

What is the default level and do I need to configure anything?
Level 5 (--no-mercy) is the default and requires nothing. It runs immediately with no warning. You can override it per-run with --level N (or an alias), or set the critique_level preference in preferences.yaml to change the baseline for all runs.
I set level 9 as my preference — do I still get asked every run?
Yes. Level 9 re-confirms on every single run regardless of any stored preference. That is by design: the behavior is severe enough that each invocation is an active choice. Decline → level 8 for that run; your stored preference stays unchanged.
Can I use level 6–9 in a real team review or a shared report?
No. Levels 6–9 are designed as a private, cathartic "destroy-me" mode intended only for the spec's own author. Level 6 in particular is described as "never appropriate for any professional setting" — not a real review, not a shared report, nothing a colleague or stakeholder will read. Keep levels 1–5 for team-visible work.
Does a higher level mean longer output?
No. Report length is controlled by critique_detail_level (concise/standard/verbose). The voice level changes tone and sharpness; the detail level changes size. They are independent knobs.
