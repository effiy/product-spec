Glossary

36 terms

One settled word replaces a paragraph of re-explanation. Definitions here are copied from the canonical sources — SKILL.md files, CLAUDE.md GATEs, and the five operating principles. Each term carries what it forbids (the wording the harness avoids) and the gate, principle, or skill that backs it.

📖
The Forbidden wording column names phrases the harness actively avoids — unmeasurable adjectives, overconfident wording, silent-reversal language. These are not style suggestions; they reflect documented design decisions in CLAUDE.md and the skill SKILL.md files.

Spec hierarchy artifacts

The five artifact levels that product-spec builds. Each level has exactly one parent (except Vision). IDs are parent-scoped and stable.

Vision The north-star narrative: why the product exists, who it is for, and what winning looks like. Captured in vision.md. Root of the whole hierarchy — no parent. All BRD goals must trace to it. "vision statement" (implies a one-liner; the artifact is richer) product-spec SKILL.md
BRD Business Requirements Document. The single BRD holds business goals (BRD-G1, BRD-G2, …), success metrics, and stakeholders. Exactly one per product; all PRDs must justify against BRD goals. multiple BRDs per product; calling a BRD a "PRD" product-spec SKILL.md
PRD Product Requirements Document. One PRD per feature area. Contains epics and traces to BRD goals. ID is slug-based (e.g. PRD-AUTH). Multiple PRDs per product are expected. one monolithic PRD for the whole product product-spec SKILL.md
Epic A deliverable slice of a PRD — large enough to span multiple stories, small enough to ship incrementally. ID is parent-scoped: PRD-AUTH-E1. Each epic contains one or more stories. equating an epic with a user story product-spec SKILL.md
Story A user-facing unit of work with acceptance criteria (AC). ID: PRD-AUTH-E1-S1. Must be INVEST-compliant (Independent / Negotiable / Valuable / Estimable / Small / Testable). Size is S | M | L — no story points or hours. story points; hour estimates; "task" (implies no user value) product-spec SKILL.md

Critique concepts

The opinion layer: non-deterministic, never gates CI, never edits the spec.

TermDefinitionForbidden wordingBacking
critique lens One of four read-only sub-agent perspectives that each critique a spec from a different angle: product (JTBD / Kano / RICE), tech (INVEST / NFRs / testability), market (competitive landscape / moat / unit-economics), craft (writing quality / term drift / vague adjectives). All four run in parallel; a consolidator merges them. mixing lens output into the spec directly; calling a lens finding a "validation error" product-spec-critique SKILL.md
voice level A 1–9 intensity scale for the critique's sarcasm and register. Default 5 (--no-mercy). Levels 1–4: artifact-only, no personal attack. Level 5: brutal but grounded. Levels 6–9: opt-in danger gate — 6 attacks the author, 7–9 escalate Vietnamese register (ông/tôi → mày/tao); level 9 adds work-targeted profanity and re-confirms on every run. "dialing up the voice" without triggering the danger gate; treating voice level as a setting that can skip the confirm product-spec-critique SKILL.md; --level flag
universal-harm floor The non-removable safety boundary that applies at ALL voice levels, even 9, even with PO consent. The critique attacks the work — never the person. Out at every level: real violence threats, protected-characteristic slurs, self-harm content, sexual content, family-targeted profanity. The target of the line — work vs person — is the criterion, not the harshness. "the floor is lowerable at level 9 with consent"; treating family-target or slurs as work-criticism product-spec-critique SKILL.md; references/voice-and-tone.md

Release concepts

How the harness packs, fingerprints, and ships the bundle — determinism and the safety filter.

TermDefinitionForbidden wordingBacking
deterministic pack The guarantee that the same inputs produce a byte-identical tar.gz regardless of when or where it is built. Achieved by: PAX format, sorted walk, mtime=0, uid=gid=0, gzip header mtime=0. A .sha256 sidecar accompanies every bundle for verification. Pinned to commit via SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH in CI. "should be the same" (without the structural guarantees above); manually running gh release create (SHA differs from CI build) release SKILL.md; Determinism section
safety filter The always-on layer inside the pack builder that unconditionally drops .env files, secrets, private keys, .git/, runtime caches, and session state from any bundle — regardless of what manifest or flags request. Non-removable via any flag. "I'll include the .env for convenience"; a flag that disables the safety filter release SKILL.md; references/safety-rules.md

Each lens reads from specific .jsonl sinks and surfaces a narrated report. ⚖️ Why a forbidden-wording column
  • Each banned phrase once caused a real operational problem — approving without a date, writing telemetry as "the product is working", treating voice level 9 as having no floor. The wrong word invites the wrong trust.
  • Evidence (IDs, file names, GATE names) is never translated — it points at the same line of source in any language.
What is the difference between validate and critique?
--validate is deterministic and reproducible — it checks structure (orphans, AC, ID integrity) then layers LLM judgment (INVEST, core-value drift). It is the CI gate. product-spec-critique is opinionated and non-deterministic — it says whether the spec is any good: why it would die in the market, whether it is a me-too, whether the writing holds up. It never gates CI.
When does a DEC get created?
A DEC-<n> is written to decisions.md via decision_register.py when: (1) the PO uses --decision to log a call, (2) a no-silent-reversal stop resolves with Keep/Change/Hybrid, or (3) the PO confirms a DEC-worthy finding from a product-spec-critique report during --apply-critique. The harness never auto-writes a DEC.
Why does telemetry always end with "Cái này KHÔNG đo được"?
The honesty gate in references/narration-contract.md mandates it. Telemetry measures machinery health — invocations, script error rates, subagent outcomes. It does not and cannot measure whether the product is winning in the market, whether users adopted a feature, or whether a decision was correct. Naming the boundary prevents the PO from over-reading the numbers.
