For non-technical product owners

product-spec

v2.4.0

Runs a phased interview, captures your product as a strictly-traceable hierarchy, validates it deterministically (structure) and by judgment (LLM), and visualizes it — all in plain language. Output lives under docs/product/; it never writes prose anywhere else.

✓ Reach for it when

  • a non-technical PO needs to capture a product as a real spec
  • you want traceability Vision → Story with acceptance criteria
  • starting a product from scratch, no code yet

✗ Not for

  • writing code — it specs, the build team builds
  • setting scope or sign-off for you — that's the PO's call
  • prose outside docs/product/
